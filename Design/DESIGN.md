## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial aesthetic tailored for a high-end digital agency and personal executive portfolio. The visual balance is calibrated at 70% restrained gallery-grade minimalism and 30% decisive, high-conversion commercial utility.

The system communicates authority, precision, and structural mastery. The target audience comprises venture-backed founders, executive design leadership, enterprise partners, and discerning product operators seeking elite product craftsmanship.

### Core Tenets
- **Architectural Discipline:** Layouts rely on strict planar alignment, razor-sharp bounding rules, and deliberate asymmetry balanced against rigid grid alignments.
- **Editorial Presence:** Large-scale, tightly tracked display typography sets an authoritative, publication-level tone paired with measured, spacious body composition.
- **Material Restraint:** No gratuitous ornament, fuzzy drop shadows, or decorative blur gradients. Depth is created through surface contrast, hairline borders, and disciplined typographic scale.
- **Inverted Finale:** The user journey culminates in a high-contrast obsidian dark section, driving visual focus directly into the engagement funnel.

## Colors

The palette employs an architectural duality: a daylight gallery aesthetic across showcase and case-study surfaces, transitioning into an executive obsidian plane for conversion modules.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#111111`):** Deep obsidian charcoal. Acts as primary ink on light surfaces, headline anchors, solid action triggers, and primary structural accents. Avoids pure digital `#000000` to preserve typographic nuance.
- **Secondary (`#737373`):** Neutral architectural slate. Governs secondary annotations, project metadata, subheadings, and secondary interaction states.
- **Tertiary (`#C2410C`):** Burnished copper/ochre. A surgical highlight color reserved exclusively for primary status indicators, key metric flags, selected interactive states, and conversion focal points.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAFAFA`):** Warm off-white substrate providing paper-like warmth, contrasted against pristine pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers.
- **Hairlines (`#E5E7EB` / `#EEEEEE`):** Crisp 1px structural partitions that define spatial planes without heavy visual mass.
- **Inverted Dark Mode (`#0D0D0D` / `#171717`):** Dedicated token set used exclusively for the final conversion section and high-impact project teasers, featuring text in `#F5F5F5` and borders in `#262626`.

All foreground-background pairings rigorously satisfy WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast ratios for body copy (minimum 7:1) and AA for display elements (minimum 4.5:1).

## Typography

The typography couples the architectural geometry of Space Grotesk with the refined cadence of Manrope and the technical rigor of JetBrains Mono.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Set with tight letter tracking (-0.02em to -0.04em) and compressed line heights. Headlines command immediate visual hierarchy and must never use faux-bold weights.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Manrope):** Structured with balanced vertical rhythm and neutral spacing. Long-form narrative blocks cap at 68 characters per line to guarantee optimal optical scanning.
- **Labels, Metrics, & System Signals (JetBrains Mono):** Set strictly in uppercase with generous tracking (+0.08em to +0.1em). Used for taxonomy tags, case-study metrics, timestamps, index numbering, and status declarations.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a modular 12-column grid anchored by generous negative space and explicit structural framing.

### Breakpoints & Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column grid, max-width 1360px container, `gutter-lg` (2.5rem / 40px), `margin-lg` (5rem / 80px). Layout leverages asymmetric spans (e.g., 5-column editorial text block paired against 7-column media frame).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px), `margin-md` (3rem / 48px). Media and copy adapt to 4:4 or full-width splits.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `gutter` (1rem / 16px), `margin` (1.5rem / 24px). Multi-column compositions collapse cleanly into single-column vertical sequences.

### Section Cadence
Full-page pacing alternates between dense information arrays and spacious atmospheric pauses:
- Hero and major case studies are bookended with `space-3xl` (6rem / 96px) padding.
- Internal component groupings use `space-md` through `space-xl` step values.
- Grid cells maintain clear spatial separation via explicit 1px outline borders rather than arbitrary white space fills.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects conventional dropped shadows and blur elevations. Depth is articulated purely through surface tiering, planar contrast, and hairline borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Substrate Level (Floor):** Neutral off-white (`#FAFAFA`) provides the foundational surface plane.
- **Card / Module Level (Tier 1):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces defined by a crisp `1px solid #EEEEEE` or `#E5E7EB` outline.
- **Active / Hover State (Tier 2):** Elevated cards shift their border to `#111111` with no blur shadow, accompanied by a subtle transform translate (`-2px, -2px`) over 180ms ease-out.
- **Inverted Module (CTA Anchor):** A recessed dark obsidian layer (`#0D0D0D`) structured with subtle `1px solid #262626` borders. High contrast light elements (`#FFFFFF` and `#C2410C`) rest directly on top of this surface.

### Border Rules
- Borders are consistently 1px and non-retina scaling.
- When two cards abut, borders collapse into a single unified shared rule to maintain structural lightness.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an uncompromising `0` (Sharp) shape philosophy across all primary layout containers, case study frames, interactive cards, and primary buttons.

### Architectural Geometry
- **Primary Components (0px):** Cards, hero viewports, input fields, image wrappers, and primary CTA buttons have sharp, unrounded right-angle corners. This preserves an architectural, drafting-table precision.
- **Subordinate Tags & Status Chips:** Metadata chips and index markers maintain crisp 0px perimeters or utilize an exact circle (`rounded-full`) exclusively for live-status pulse indicators (e.g., 6px operational availability dot).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Light Surface):** Solid `#111111` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 0px border radius, uppercase `label-md` JetBrains Mono, 16px horizontal / 14px vertical padding. Hover state: background shifts to `#C2410C`.
- **Secondary Button (Light Surface):** Transparent background, `1px solid #111111` border, `#111111` text. Hover state: fills `#111111` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Inverted Primary CTA (Dark Section):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` background with `#0D0D0D` text. Hover state: background shifts to `#C2410C` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Editorial Case Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, framed in `1px solid #E5E7EB`. Contains an edge-to-edge 16:10 aspect ratio visual display, separated from metadata by a 1px internal divider. Contains title, client tag, year in `label-sm`, and key deliverable metric highlighted with `#C2410C`.
- **Interactive State:** Hover invokes a 1px border shift to `#111111` and an instantaneous corner arrow glyph transition (`↗`).

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Specification Chip:** Outlined in `1px solid #E5E7EB`, padding `4px 8px`, `label-sm` JetBrains Mono, `#737373` text. No background fill.
- **Status Indicator:** Transparent container with a leading 6px circular dot (`#C2410C` for "Available for Q3/Q4"), paired with `label-sm` copy in `#111111`.

### Form Controls
- **Text Inputs & Textareas:** Framed in `1px solid #E5E7EB`, `#FFFFFF` surface on light backgrounds, or `1px solid #262626` on inverted dark surfaces. Typography in `body-md` Manrope.
- **Focus State:** 1px crisp stroke in `#111111` (or `#FFFFFF` on dark mode); no external glow or halo ring. Labels sit persistently above the input in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Lists & Key Metrics
- **Metric Row:** Large numeral in `display-xl` Space Grotesk, immediately paired with an uppercase JetBrains Mono description stacked directly underneath or alongside, punctuated by a hairline divider.
- **Index Table:** Horizontal rows with `1px solid #EEEEEE` top and bottom boundaries, featuring project title, role, year, and a live link trigger.

### Inverted Conversion Module (Final CTA)
- Full bleed surface in `#0D0D0D` featuring `headline-lg` text in `#FFFFFF`, supported by a direct calendar booking form or contact trigger. Accent touches in `#C2410C` drive instantaneous visual priority.
